Data Deletion Instructions

Claude API, a private publishing tool run by Fantasy Lights (Terrence Phiri). Last updated 22 October 2026.

Claude API stores only the page access tokens granted by page administrators and the reports made from them. To have that data deleted:

  1. Remove the app from your account: on Facebook, go to Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations, select Claude API and choose Remove. This revokes its access immediately.
  2. To have stored tokens and reports erased too, open an issue at github.com/fantasylights/tp-privacy naming the page. They will be deleted within 30 days, and the issue will be answered to confirm it.
