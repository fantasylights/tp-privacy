Privacy Policy

Claude API, a private publishing tool run by Fantasy Lights (Terrence Phiri), Harare, Zimbabwe. Last updated 22 October 2026.

What this app is

Claude API is an internal tool. It publishes posts and reads performance statistics for Facebook Pages, Instagram accounts and related pages that Fantasy Lights manages for itself and for the organisations it works with. It is not offered to the public, and nobody signs up to it.

What data it uses

The app uses only the access tokens granted by the page administrators who authorised it, plus the page content and insights those tokens allow: posts, media, comments, follower counts and engagement statistics. It does not collect data from people who visit or interact with those pages beyond what Meta shows to the page administrators.

How the data is used

Data is used only to publish content the page owners approved and to prepare internal performance reports for those pages. It is not sold, shared with advertisers or used for any other purpose.

Storage and security

Tokens and reports are stored on a single private computer controlled by Fantasy Lights. They are not uploaded to third-party services.
